The problem

Money sent home is rarely for one thing

Someone in London supporting family in Lagos covers rent, a sibling’s school fees and upkeep in the same month.

Today they send one lump sum and hope it’s divided the way they meant, or make several transfers and pay a fee on each.

No app lets the sender say what the money is for at the moment they send it. Splits does.

About Splits

Splits is a payments company built around one idea: when you send money, you should be able to say what it’s for, and it should arrive that way.

We started with families sending money home, where a single transfer usually has to cover several needs. Next we’re adding card and bank funding, cash-out to bank accounts and mobile money, and new corridors across Europe and Asia.
